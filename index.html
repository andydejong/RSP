HOW TO PLAY

Move diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. Jump over a piece to capture it; you may chain jumps. Your piece becomes a king on the far row and can move in either direction.

R Rock beats rock / scissorsS Scissors beats scissors / paperP Paper beats paper / rock

Small Game: the first legal capture wins. Big Game: capture one of every type, or wipe out one type entirely.
